Briefing

Hazard data for Wellington is published by three agencies, in seven themes, across 67 separate services.

This board pulls all of them into one index. Each entry is a live reference into a public ArcGIS service — hazard extents, exposure models and climate time series maintained by council and national science agencies. Colour carries the meaning: a card's leading edge names the tier that publishes it, and each theme owns a channel hue used consistently across the map, filters and charts.

Publishing tier — the stripe on each card

  • City — Wellington City Council
  • Regional — Greater Wellington
  • National — GNS & agencies

Data sources

Wellington City Council — city hazard and exposure layers Greater Wellington Regional Council — regional flood and coastal data GNS Science — active faults and national geohazard models
