About this board Who publishes the data and how the catalogue is structured

Hazard data for Wellington is published by three agencies, in seven themes, across 67 separate services.

This board pulls all of them into one index. Each entry is a live reference into a public ArcGIS service — hazard extents, exposure models and climate time series maintained by council and national science agencies. Colour carries the meaning: a card's leading edge names the tier that publishes it, and each hazard theme owns a channel hue — carried in the read-out above, and in the map, filters and charts as they land.

Data sources

Wellington City Council — city hazard and exposure layers Greater Wellington Regional Council — regional flood and coastal data GNS Science — active faults and national geohazard models
